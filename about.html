About Our Children's Hospital

Krushil Children's Hospital aims to provide compassionate, child-friendly healthcare for infants, children and adolescents in a welcoming environment. Our focus is on treating every child with care, respect and attention while helping families understand their child's healthcare needs.

Our Mission

To support children's health and wellbeing by making quality, child-focused healthcare accessible to families.
